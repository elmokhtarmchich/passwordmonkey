# Donate to PasswordMonkey

## Why Donate?

PasswordMonkey is and will always be free to use. However, development, hosting, and maintenance require resources. Your donations help us:

- Keep the service running and reliable
- Develop new features and improvements
- Maintain security and privacy standards
- Support ongoing development
- Keep the service completely free for everyone

## How You Can Help

### Financial Support

Donations help cover:
- Server hosting and infrastructure
- Development tools and services
- Security audits and monitoring
- Domain registration and maintenance
- Community support and documentation

### Other Ways to Contribute

- Star our GitHub repository
- Share PasswordMonkey with others
- Contribute code or documentation
- Report bugs or suggest improvements
- Spread awareness about password security

## Donation Options

We accept donations through various platforms. Choose the method that works best for you.

## Privacy Promise

- We never store payment information
- Donations are processed securely by payment providers
- Your donation history is private
- No recurring donations without your consent

## Thank You

Thank you for considering supporting PasswordMonkey. Your contribution helps us keep the service free, secure, and available to everyone.
