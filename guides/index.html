The Complete Guide to Password Safety

Last updated: October 2026

Passwords are the keys to your digital life. This guide walks you through everything — why random passwords matter, where to save them, how to recover when you lose access, and how to secure your most important accounts — step by step, in plain language.

Start here: why random passwords

Reused passwords are the biggest risk to your online accounts. If one site is breached, every account using that password is at risk. Random passwords break that chain.
