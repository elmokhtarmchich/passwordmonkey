## 1. Purpose

Document an ethical link-building strategy for PasswordMonkey's free password generator and password-education guides. This document defines target outreach categories, three email templates, a tracking-sheet template, and a "never do" list. It is for planning only — no emails are sent, no source pages are modified, and no outreach is performed as a result of this document.

All outreach must respect PasswordMonkey's core privacy invariant: password generation stays client-side (Web Crypto API). Nothing generated is logged, stored, or transmitted.

---

## 2. Target categories

| # | Category | Where to look | Example page types |
|---|----------|---------------|--------------------|
| 1 | Libraries (public library resource pages) | City/county library websites, "resources" or "digital literacy" sections | Library online safety pages, parent/teen resource guides |
| 2 | Schools (K-12 digital literacy pages) | School district sites, teacher resource portals, library-media centers | Digital citizenship curricula, online safety lesson plans |
| 3 | Universities (student resource centers, IT security pages) | University IT/help desk pages, student affairs, library guides | Student tech resource centers, campus cybersecurity guides |
| 4 | Nonprofits (consumer protection, digital rights) | Consumer advocacy orgs, digital rights groups, EFF-style resource lists | Identity theft prevention guides, digital privacy toolkits |
| 5 | Digital-literacy resource pages | Senior centers, community tech programs, adult education | Computer basics classes, internet safety workshops |
| 6 | Senior-safety resource pages | Elder fraud prevention hubs, AARP-style resources, aging services | Scam alert pages, senior online safety guides |

Outreach is one contact per site, one template per contact, with a single follow-up max.

### Template rules

- Placeholder fields in ALL CAPS with brackets: [RECIPIENT NAME], [SITE NAME], [RESOURCE PAGE URL], [ARTICLE URL], [PAGE], [EMAIL], [YOUR NAME]
- Warm, plain language (~grade 8 reading level)
- No pressure, no demands, no urgency tactics
- Every template includes clear unsubscribe/opt-out language
- Sending address placeholder: [EMAIL]
- One follow-up maximum, at least 7 days after the first email

## 5. "Never do" list

- Buying links
- Link networks / private blog networks (PBNs)
- Link exchanges at scale
- Repeated exact-match anchor text
- Keyword-stuffed press releases
- Comment spam or forum drops
- Expired-domain redirects
- Seeding fake mentions or reviews
- Any tactic that violates search engine spam policies

---

## 6. Additional rules

- Use rel="sponsored" on any affiliate links
- Never pay for links
- Never exchange money, goods, or services for links
- Disclose any affiliate relationships clearly
